The vision

Your syllabus becomes the storyline.

The goal isn't a quiz with a game wrapped around it. It's a complete, engaging adventure where understanding the material is how you move forward.

01 / LEARN

Start with your material.

Upload a course outline. The planned engine will identify the concepts, their order, and what mastery looks like.

02 / MATCH

Find the right world.

Locked will choose the most suitable maps and story modes from a curated library, based on the topic and learner.

03 / PLAY

Earn every breakthrough.

Progress through a full story by solving challenges that require deep thinking, experimentation, and understanding.
